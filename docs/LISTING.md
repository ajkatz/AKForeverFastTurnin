# Listing text - AKForeverFastTurnin

**Summary (one line):** The clicks of a repeatable hand-in, made for you: sealed Waylaid Crates, Craftsman's Writs and any repeatable quest are accepted and handed in when you talk to the NPC - and escort quests are started and joined.

## Description

**Fast Turn-in** - for WoW: Forever.

Talk to the intake NPC with a sealed crate in your bags and it is handed in: no quest list, no Accept, no
Continue, no Complete Quest. One talk per crate - the game closes the NPC's window after each hand-in.

- **Sealed crates.** The "A Sealed Crate" quests at the intake NPC (Dokimi west of the Crossroads, or the
  Alliance's in Redridge Mountains). A crate's quest is only accepted when that crate is in your bags, so
  your quest log is never filled with crates you do not have.
- **Craftsman's Writs.** Accepted when you open the writ, handed in when you talk to its customer.
- **Any quest the game calls repeatable**, at any NPC.
- **Escorts are started for you.** Talk to an escort's NPC - Corporal Keeshan, the OOX robots, Willix,
  Tooga and some forty more - and the quest is picked and accepted. When someone in your party starts one,
  the game's "would you like to as well?" is answered yes. Handing an escort in stays yours.
- **Not in Razorfen Downs.** "Extinguishing the Idol" is never started and never joined for you: accepting
  it sets off the fight at the idol. "Jail Break!" in Blackrock Depths is not started for you either.

Every other quest is left alone, every step of it.

**Where it stops:** Shift held while you talk to the NPC; a choice of rewards; a hand-in that costs gold;
anything that is not complete yet.

## Commands

`/akft` (or `/fastturnin`):

- `/akft` - whether it is on, and the commands
- `/akft off`, `/akft on` - switch it; kept between sessions
- `/akft escorts off`, `/akft escorts on` - starting and joining escorts
- `/akft log` - what it did, what it left alone, and why
