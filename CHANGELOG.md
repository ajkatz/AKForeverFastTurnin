# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Talk to the intake NPC (Dokimi in The Barrens, or the Alliance's in Redridge) with sealed crates in your
  bags: "A Sealed Crate" is picked, accepted and handed in for you. One talk per crate: the game closes
  the window after each hand-in.
- A Craftsman's Writ is accepted when you open it and handed in when you talk to its customer.
- Any other quest the game calls repeatable is accepted and handed in the same way.
- It stops for a choice of rewards, a hand-in that costs gold, and anything that is not complete. A sealed
  crate quest is only accepted when its crate is in your bags.
- Escorts are started for you: talk to the NPC and the quest is picked and accepted (some forty-five of
  the old world's escort and stand-guard quests). When someone in your party starts one, the game's "would
  you like to as well?" is answered yes. Handing an escort in stays yours. "Extinguishing the Idol" in
  Razorfen Downs is never started and never joined; "Jail Break!" in Blackrock Depths is not started for
  you. `/akft escorts off` leaves them all to you.
- Hold Shift while talking to an NPC to do it yourself. `/akft on`, `/akft off`, `/akft log`.
